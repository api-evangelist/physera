Overview A leading department store retailer with a predominantly non-desk, hourly workforce needed a chronic care partner that could drive meaningful engagement and outcomes, and help manage GLP-1 spend, without adding complexity. Life for the company's associates is anything but linear. Shifts change, priorities collide, and for a large portion of the population, their cardiometabolic health took a back seat.
